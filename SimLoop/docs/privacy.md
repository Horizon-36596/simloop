# Privacy

SimLoop is a library. It runs on your computer and your robot and sends nothing anywhere.

This documentation site counts page views with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), so we can
see which pages teams read.

- It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
- For each page view it records the page, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen
  size, and your country. GoatCounter works out the country from your IP address and then drops the
  address.
- It cannot tell who you are or follow you to other sites.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps the routine request logs any website has, such as
your IP address.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Horizon-36596/simloop/issues).
